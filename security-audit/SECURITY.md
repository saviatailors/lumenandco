# Security Policy & Responsible Disclosure — Lumen & Co

Lumen & Co takes the security of our platform, our clients, and their business data seriously. We welcome responsible security researchers and partners who report security vulnerabilities to us.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in Lumen & Co web properties or APIs:
- **Email:** `security@lumenandco.in` or `sahitsai6@gmail.com`
- **PGP/Encrypted:** Available upon request.
- **Response SLA:** We acknowledge all security reports within 24 hours.

## Disclosure Policy
- Please provide detailed steps to reproduce the issue.
- Give us reasonable time to remediate the vulnerability before public disclosure.
- Do not perform destructive attacks (DDoS, deleting client data, or social engineering).
- Never access or exfiltrate private client data or confidential leads.

## Supported Scope
- `https://www.lumenandco.in`
- All subdomains and API endpoints under `lumenandco.in`
